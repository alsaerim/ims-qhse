About ISO Radar

ISO Radar is a quality, health & safety and environmental management platform built around ISO standards — one place for the daily work of quality teams.

What it does

  • Inspections and audits with checklists; nonconformities linked to root cause analysis and followed until closed.
  • Risk register, document control, training and competence, annual plan and KPIs.
  • Gap analysis for ISO 9001, 45001, 14001 and 27001, plus custom standards.
  • An offline-capable field app and audit-ready PDF reports.

Who it is for

Leadership that needs one view of compliance across projects, quality managers who follow findings to closure, and inspectors in the field.

Two languages

The whole system works in Arabic and English, and every user can switch at any time.
