FAQ

Questions? Answers.

Can I try before I pay?

Yes — the 14-day free trial includes every feature, and no credit card is needed.

Which ISO standards are supported?

ISO 9001, 45001, 14001 and 27001, and you can add custom standards with their own requirements and assessment questions.

Does the field app work offline?

Yes. Inspections and photos are saved on the device and sync automatically when you're back online.

Is the system available in Arabic and English?

Yes, the whole system is fully bilingual, and each user can switch with the language button at the top of the page.

Can it connect to our existing systems?

On Professional and Enterprise you can integrate with Odoo, ERPNext and SAP over API.

Are there hidden fees?

No. The price includes every feature in the plan, and you can cancel any time.
